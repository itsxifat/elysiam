# M57 · Staff activity: what each person did, how long, and their mistakes

The owner's request of 2026-11-10: "an advanced system to monitor staff — a detailed activity
record, not only how many orders handled: how many pieces, how many messages, calls, hours
talked, everything; for the packing staff how many packed and returns closed; and detect staff
mistakes. A dedicated page and feature."

Status: **approved 2026-11-10**, to build now (ahead of M54b, M55 CRM leads and M56
after-sales, which keep their numbers). The owner's answers to the plan:

- Staff see their own page **and a comparison table** of their colleagues on the same page.
- Mistakes **can feed the bonus rules**, behind a brand switch that turns it off and leaves
  review only.

Branch `m57-staff-activity`, in its own worktree (another session's courier work is uncommitted
in the main checkout). Decision rows are D-1000 to D-1022: main took D-976 for a label fix and other branches use D-974 and D-975, so M57's rows were moved clear of them.

## Scope

One place in the console, **Staff activity**, that answers for any person and any period:
what they did, how much, how fast, when they were working, and what went wrong.

It is built from what each module already records with the person on it. Nothing is counted
twice and no figure has a second copy to drift:

| Desk       | Already recorded, per person                                       | Source                                                                                      |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Orders     | created, taken, confirmed, cancelled, edited, returned (who acted) | `orders.order_events.actor_user_id`, `hr.desk_events`                                       |
| Orders     | pieces and value of what they confirmed                            | `orders.order_lines`                                                                        |
| Orders     | verification call attempts and outcomes, call-back times           | `orders.call_attempts.called_by`                                                            |
| Orders     | auto-assign offers accepted / declined / timed out                 | `orders.order_offers`                                                                       |
| Inbox      | conversations taken, messages sent, first-reply time, notes        | `inbox.messages.sent_by`, `inbox.conversations`, `inbox.notes`                              |
| Calls      | calls in and out, answered, missed, talk seconds, wrap-ups         | `telephony.calls.user_id`, `talk_seconds`, `wrapped_up_by_user_id`                          |
| Packing    | orders packed, pieces scanned, pack time, wrong scans              | `packing.sessions`, `packing.scans.scanned_by` + `outcome`                                  |
| Packing    | lots closed, handover scans, labels printed                        | `packing.lot_closures`, `packing.handover_scans`, `packing.print_jobs`                      |
| Returns    | returns received and closed, short or damaged                      | `courier.return_receipts.received_by`, `advances.order_returns.recorded_by`, return station |
| Everything | every audited action                                               | `audit.audit_log.actor_user_id`                                                             |

The one thing **not** recorded today is time: `team.presence` keeps only each person's last
state, so "hours in the ERP, active vs idle" cannot be answered for yesterday. This milestone
adds a presence history from the console's existing heartbeat (M41). Hours start counting from
the day it is deployed; there is no earlier history to recover.

### What the page shows

1. **Team** (`/staff-activity`): one row per person for the period (today, yesterday, this
   week, this month, custom up to 92 days), filtered by desk (sales, inbox, hub) and role.
   Columns, grouped and switchable: hours online / active / idle / paused, first sign-in and
   last activity of the day; orders taken, confirmed, cancelled, pieces confirmed, delivery rate
   of what they confirmed; messages sent, conversations, median first reply; calls, talk time;
   packed, pieces packed, median pack time; returns received and closed; mistakes and mistakes
   per 100 actions. Sortable, with the team median under each column, and a CSV export.
2. **Person** (`/staff-activity/$userId`): metric cards against the team median and the
   previous period; activity by day (bars per desk); an hour × weekday heatmap of when they
   work; their open work right now (from the team board).
3. **Timeline** (a tab of the person page): every action in order, minute by minute, each
   linked to its order, conversation, call or packing video: "10:42 confirmed #10233 (3 pcs)",
   "10:44 call to 01•••••789, 2 m 10 s, reached", "10:51 replied in a Messenger conversation".
   Gaps where they were idle or away are shown as gaps.
4. **Mistakes** (`/staff-activity/mistakes` and a tab of the person page): every detected
   mistake with who, when, what, and the evidence link. An org admin marks each one
   _confirmed_ or _not a mistake_ with a note; counts and rates use confirmed plus unreviewed,
   never dismissed.
5. **Settings** (`/settings/staff-activity`): the thresholds below, per brand.

6. **Comparison** (on every person's page, and the whole of what staff see of others): a table
   of the people on the same desk for the same period, with headline figures only (hours
   active, orders confirmed, pieces, messages, calls and talk time, packed, returns closed,
   mistakes per 100 actions) and their rank, the viewer's own row highlighted. A brand setting
   chooses whether staff see colleagues' names or only "Colleague 1, 2…" with their own row
   named; default names.

A sales or warehouse person sees **their own** page ("My activity") with the comparison table;
never another person's timeline, mistake details or person page. Org admin and HR see everyone.

### Mistakes in the bonus rules (behind a switch)

- A brand setting **Mistakes reduce bonuses**, off by default. Off: nothing below applies and
  the bonus screens say "review only".
- A bonus rule may carry an optional `mistakes` block:
  `{"per_mistake_poisha": 5000, "kinds": ["wrong_item_shipped", "address_not_verified"],
"max_poisha": 100000}`. Each **confirmed** mistake of those kinds in the rule's period takes
  `per_mistake_poisha` off that person's bonus, up to `max_poisha`, never below zero: a
  mistake can lose a bonus, never cut salary. Unreviewed and dismissed mistakes never count
  for pay.
- The deduction is part of the pure `evaluateBonus` (table tests), shown in the rule's preview
  line by line, and frozen in the award when the payroll run's checker approves it: the
  mistake keys counted are stored with the award, so a review changed afterwards never moves a
  bonus already approved (it shows on the next period's preview as a note instead).
- HR reads confirmed mistakes through `StaffActivityApi.confirmedMistakes`; the switch is read
  at evaluation time and recorded on the award.

### Mistakes it detects (rules in `packages/domain`, each with a stable key)

| Mistake                       | Whose              | Rule                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wrong piece scanned           | packer             | a scan with outcome `wrong_item`, `wrong_variant` or `over` (caught by the system, shown as low severity)   |
| Wrong item shipped            | packer             | a return with reason `wrong_item`: the person who completed that order's packing                            |
| Address or phone not verified | confirmer          | a return for `wrong_address` or `customer_unreachable` on an order confirmed with no _reached_ call attempt |
| Confirmed without a call      | confirmer          | confirmed with no call attempt at all, when the brand requires verification                                 |
| Cancelled after confirming    | confirmer          | an order they confirmed, cancelled before handover                                                          |
| Call-back missed              | order owner        | a `call_back_at` that passed by more than N minutes with no new attempt                                     |
| Slow first reply              | conversation owner | first reply later than N minutes after the customer's message, in working hours                             |
| Conversation left unanswered  | conversation owner | a customer message unanswered for more than N minutes while they held it                                    |
| Missed call                   | extension holder   | an inbound call to their extension, unanswered, while they were active                                      |
| Call not wrapped up           | caller             | an answered call with no wrap-up after N minutes                                                            |
| Offer left to time out        | offered person     | an auto-assign offer that expired while they were active                                                    |
| Packed without video          | packer             | a session completed with video required and no video                                                        |

The thresholds (N) are brand settings with defaults (D-rows below). Each rule is a pure,
table-tested function. A mistake is computed when the page is read, never stored, so a rule
change applies to the past too. Only the review (confirmed / dismissed, note, who, when) is
stored, keyed by the mistake's stable key.

### Out of scope

- Anything outside the ERP tab: browsing, screens, keystrokes, CCTV (D-292 removed the
  extension; the console only reports its own tab's state, as in M41).
- Salary deductions or fines from mistakes: a mistake only ever reduces a bonus, to zero at
  most.
- Cross-brand views (each brand's ERP is its own, M31); the mother ERP gets nothing new.
- Notifying a person when a mistake is recorded against them (follow-up).
- Presence history before deployment.
- Message bodies, call recordings or full phone numbers in this module's responses: the timeline
  links to the conversation, call or order, which keep their own permissions.

## Data model (migration 0174, schema `staff_activity`)

- `staff_activity.presence_spans`: `org_id`, `user_id`, `state` (`active` | `idle` |
  `paused`), `started_at`, `ended_at`. The team service's heartbeat extends the open span or,
  on a state change or a gap longer than the away threshold, closes it and opens a new one.
  One write per heartbeat, in the transaction that already updates `team.presence`.
  Constraint: `ended_at >= started_at`; index `(org_id, user_id, started_at)`; an exclusion
  constraint so one person's spans never overlap. Kept 400 days (a nightly purge job).
- `staff_activity.mistake_reviews`: `org_id`, `mistake_key` (unique per org), `kind`,
  `user_id`, `status` (`confirmed` | `dismissed`), `note` (≤ 500), `reviewed_by`,
  `reviewed_at`. A review can be changed; each change audited.
- `staff_activity.settings`: one row per org with the thresholds.
- RLS ENABLE and FORCE with the standard policy on all three (invariant 4); grants to
  `erp_app` only.
- Indexes added where a source query would scan: `(org_id, scanned_by, scanned_at)` on
  `packing.scans`, `(org_id, sent_by, occurred_at)` on `inbox.messages`, `(org_id,
called_by, called_at)` on `orders.call_attempts`, `(org_id, actor_user_id, occurred_at)` on
  `orders.order_events`, whichever are missing after checking each table's existing indexes.

`presence_spans` is written from the team module (it owns presence), so the table sits in the
`team` schema instead if the module-boundary check prefers that; decided in the first slice.

## Module and boundaries (invariant 12)

A new module `staff-activity`. It reads, never writes, other modules, through new methods on
their existing `*.api.ts` ports, each returning per-person facts for an org and a period:

- `OrdersApi.staffFacts` (events, pieces, value, call attempts, offers, returns by reason)
- `InboxApi.staffFacts` (messages sent, conversations, first replies, unanswered spans)
- `TelephonyApi.staffFacts` (calls, talk seconds, missed, wrap-ups)
- `PackingApi.staffFacts` (sessions, scans by outcome, lots, handovers, prints, return station)
- `CourierApi.staffFacts` (return receipts)
- `TeamApi.presenceSpans` and `TeamApi.board` (the live part)
- `AuditApi` for the timeline's remaining actions

Each port also has a `staffTimeline(orgId, userId, from, to)` returning that person's items
with an id, time, kind and link. `staff-activity` imports those modules; none of them import
it, so no cycle (orders already imports team; team does not import orders).

## Domain (`packages/domain/src/staff-activity/`)

- `presence.ts`: folding heartbeats into spans; hours online, active, idle, paused per Dhaka
  day. **Property tests:** spans never overlap; active + idle + paused + offline equals the
  period exactly; a heartbeat gap beyond the away threshold always ends a span.
- `metrics.ts`: per-person and team-median figures from the ports' facts (medians
  nearest-rank, as D-276).
- `mistakes.ts`: one function per rule, `(facts, settings) → Mistake[]`, each with a
  deterministic key `kind:sourceId`. **Table-driven tests** per rule, including edges
  (reassigned order, call-back moved, conversation handed over, working hours).
- `timeline.ts`: merging the ports' items, grouping idle gaps.

## API (`/v1/staff-activity/...`, CASL subject `StaffActivity`)

| Route                                                           | What                            | Who                                  |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| `GET /v1/staff-activity/team?from&to&desk`                      | the team table                  | org_admin, hr                        |
| `GET /v1/staff-activity/people/:userId?from&to`                 | person summary, by-day, heatmap | org_admin, hr; anyone for themselves |
| `GET /v1/staff-activity/people/:userId/timeline?from&to&cursor` | the timeline, paged             | same                                 |
| `GET /v1/staff-activity/mistakes?from&to&userId&kind&status`    | mistakes                        | same (own only for staff)            |
| `PUT /v1/staff-activity/mistakes/:key/review`                   | confirm or dismiss with a note  | org_admin                            |
| `GET/PUT /v1/staff-activity/settings`                           | thresholds                      | org_admin                            |
| `GET /v1/staff-activity/team.csv`                               | export                          | org_admin, hr                        |

Responses go through `serializeFor()`; no cost field exists in them. Phone numbers are masked
(`01•••••789`). Every route is in the route matrix with fixtures.

## Assumptions (new decisions.md rows, D-1000 onward)

- Who sees what: org_admin and HR all staff; everyone else themselves only. Branch managers
  see nobody else's (no branch dimension on desk work today).
- Mistake attribution as in the table above, especially: a `wrong_address` /
  `customer_unreachable` return is the confirmer's mistake only when no call reached the
  customer; a `wrong_item` return is the packer's.
- Default thresholds: call-back missed after 30 min; slow first reply 15 min; unanswered 60
  min; wrap-up within 10 min; working hours 09:00–22:00 Dhaka (outside them the inbox rules
  pause).
- Counts and rates include unreviewed and confirmed mistakes, never dismissed ones.
- An active hour stays D-276's for the existing HR productivity report; the new page shows
  presence-based hours alongside, labelled differently, so the existing bonus inputs don't
  change.
- Presence spans kept 400 days.

## Risks

- **Speed:** the team table reads six modules for up to 92 days. Mitigated by the indexes
  above and per-port aggregate SQL (no row fetching); measured with `pnpm bench
staff-activity` at §29's sizes, budget p95 ≤ 1.5 s for 60 people × 31 days. If it misses,
  a nightly per-person-per-day rollup is the fallback (a follow-up, not a second source of
  truth for today).
- **Fairness of attribution:** a rule can blame the wrong person (a reassigned order, a
  handed-over chat). Every rule is tested on those cases, and the review step exists so a
  supervisor can dismiss it.
- **Heartbeat load:** one extra write per heartbeat per person; the span update is a single
  conditional UPDATE, measured in the bench.

## Acceptance checks

1. On seeded activity across every desk, each team-table figure equals a direct SQL query on
   the source tables (integration test, real Postgres).
2. Presence property tests (above), and an integration test that a heartbeat stream with an
   idle stretch, a pause and a gap produces the expected hours.
3. Every mistake rule passes its table-driven tests; on seeded data each detected mistake links
   to its evidence; a dismissed mistake leaves the counts and keeps its review and audit row.
4. A sales person reading another person's page, timeline or mistakes gets 403; the route
   matrix covers every route; a response sweep finds no full phone number, message body or
   cost field.
5. `pnpm bench staff-activity` p95 within budget, recorded in the report.
6. A stack spec (`staff-activity.stack.ts`) through the real stack with screenshots in
   `docs/review/M57/`: team table, person page, timeline, a mistake confirmed and one
   dismissed, My activity as a sales person.
7. With the switch on, a rule with a `mistakes` block pays less by exactly the confirmed
   mistakes (table tests, a payroll run on real Postgres), never below zero; a review changed
   after approval leaves the award as approved; with the switch off the same run pays the full
   bonus.
8. A sales person's page shows the comparison table with colleagues' headline figures only,
   and names hidden when the brand setting says so.
9. `pnpm verify` green; ledger-auditor (permissions, tenancy, payroll); acceptance-auditor.

## As built (changes from the plan above)

- `presence_spans` sits in the `team` schema (the team module owns presence and writes it); no
  exclusion constraint (it would need `btree_gist`): the presence row lock and the tests hold the
  no-overlap rule, a trigger lets only a span's end move. Paused is a flag on a span (D-1005).
- Another person's page, timeline or mistakes answer **404** (the scope's no-oracle answer), the
  team table 403 `STAFF_ACTIVITY_TEAM_ONLY`. Settings are org_admin's (`update`), hr reads only
  (D-1012).
- The timeline is read a day at a time in the console (the API takes up to 7 days, 1,500 lines).
- The inbox hands over its counts and waits in one call (`InboxApi.staffFacts`), one windowed
  pass (D-1008); every module reads in its own transaction at once (D-1010). Team table p95 1.20 s.
- The bench is a vitest file run by `pnpm bench staff-activity` (Nest needs the test build's
  decorator metadata, which tsx does not emit).
- One nav entry, Staff activity, for everyone: supervisors land on the team table, staff on My
  activity. Settings live in the section's own tab, not Settings.
- One console route, `/staff-activity`: the mistakes and settings are `?view=mistakes|settings`
  and a person's page `?who=<user id>`, and the screens check their params in their own chunk.
  Every extra route put a component loader and a preload list into the first download, which
  sat at 249.8 KB of its 250 KB budget; with one route it is 249.98 KB.
- Decisions D-1000 to D-1022. Migrations 0174 to 0177.
